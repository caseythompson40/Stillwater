Still Water

Make the ripples meet

How to play

  • Ice: tap the water to drop a pebble. One ripple can't break ice, so drop two whose rings cross on it.
  • Lanterns: they need three crests at once. Drop three pebbles around one, quickly. Its gold dots count the crests.
  • Seed pods: when a pod's shadow shrinks, tap the shadow. Your pebble lands with it and the dark wave cancels out.
  • Paper boats: they can't be broken. Push them with ripples into a rock or the bank.
  • Today's Pond: a new 90-second pond every day, the same for everyone. Your first try counts.

Questions or problems

Please open an issue on GitHub and describe what happened, with your iPhone model and iOS version if you can.

Common fixes:

  • No sound: Still Water follows the ring/silent switch. Turn silent mode off, and check Sound and Music under Settings on the title screen.
  • Lost progress: scores and progress are kept only on your phone, so deleting the app removes them.

Privacy

Still Water collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
