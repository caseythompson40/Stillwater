Privacy Policy

Still Water for iPhone · updated November 20, 2026

The short version

Still Water collects no data about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your best scores, how far you got in the journey, your Today's Pond results and streak, and your sound, music and haptics settings. These are kept only on your device, are never sent anywhere, and are deleted when you delete the app.

Sharing

If you tap Share after Today's Pond, the game hands a line of text (the date, your score and your petals) to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you and the app you choose.

Children

Because the game collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions are welcome on the support page.
